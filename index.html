Questions people ask before booking

How do you charge and how do I pay?

Fixed price per scope, quoted in USD. 50% up front, 50% on delivery, or full amount held in milestone escrow for first-time projects. Settlement in USD via bank transfer or USDT/USDC — whichever your finance team prefers.

What time zone are you in, and does it matter?

I'm in GMT+3:30 and I keep a 4-hour overlap with European mornings and a 2-hour overlap with US East Coast mornings. Async by default: written updates every 48 hours, calls scheduled inside your working day.

What do you need from me to start?

A sample export (anonymised is fine), one sentence on the decision the report should support, and access to whoever answers questions about the data. That's it — I don't need your whole warehouse.

Do you replace our analyst?

No. I take the recurring, well-defined work off their plate so they can do the ambiguous work. Most of my clients are agencies whose analysts are booked on client delivery, not internal reporting.

What if my data is a mess?

It usually is. The cleanup sprint exists for exactly that, and it's priced so you can find out what you're dealing with before committing to a bigger build.

No pitch deck, no discovery workshop. One file, one short call, one honest answer about whether it's worth fixing.
